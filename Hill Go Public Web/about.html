Our Origin Story

Founded in 2016, HillGo started with a simple mission: make urban mobility accessible, safe, and efficient for everyone. What began as a ride-hailing startup has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem serving millions daily.

Today, we operate across 500+ cities, processing over 130 million deliveries and connecting 200,000+ businesses to their customers.
