General Questions

HillGo is an all-in-one platform for urban mobility and logistics. We offer ride-hailing, food delivery, and parcel courier services through a single app and merchant portal.
Download the HillGo app from the App Store or Google Play, enter your phone number, verify with OTP, and complete your profile. For merchants, register at our merchant portal.
HillGo operates across 64 districts with home delivery in every district. Ride and food services are available in all major metropolitan areas.

Delivery Information

Open the HillGo app, go to More > Tracking, and enter your consignment ID. You can also use the Track Order section on our homepage.
Same city: 24 hours. Inter-city: 48-72 hours. Express same-day delivery is available in metropolitan areas with entry before 12:00 PM.

Payment

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, mobile banking, and HillGo Pay. Merchants can receive COD settlements via bank transfer or mobile wallet.
COD charge is 0.5% within metropolitan city and 1% for deliveries outside the metro area.

Partnering

Merchants need a registered business, valid ID, and bank or mobile wallet account. Drivers need a valid license, vehicle registration, and background check clearance.
