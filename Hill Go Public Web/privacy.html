1. Introduction

HillGo Inc. ("HillGo," "we," "us") operates ride-hailing, food delivery, and parcel logistics services. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information when you use our apps, websites, and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways:

Direct Data

  • • Name and contact details
  • • Account credentials
  • • Payment information
  • • Social media login info

Automated Data

  • • Log and usage data
  • • Device information
  • • Location information
  • • Tracking cookies

3. How We Use Data

We use your information to:

  1. 1 Provide and improve rides, food, and parcel services
  2. 2 Process payments and send service notifications
  3. 3 Ensure safety, prevent fraud, and comply with law

4. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

We share information with drivers, merchants, and delivery partners only as needed to fulfill your requests. Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access.
